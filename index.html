Guidelines for TAs

How to check in and check out
  1. Open the attendance app with the blue button above, or scan the QR code at the office. Sign in with your East West University Google account.
  2. Allow location access when your browser asks. You must be inside the Department office(Room 236) area.
  3. Tap Check In. Your status is recorded as On Time or Late (see the time rules).
  4. Stay for your scheduled office hours. A timer on the page shows how long you have been checked in.
  5. When your last slot ends, tap Check Out while you are still at the office.
Time rules and grace periods
Check-in opens10 minutes before your first slot starts
On timeCheck in up to 10 minutes after your first slot starts
LateCheck in more than 10 minutes after your first slot starts
Check-in closesWhen your last slot ends
Check-outTap Check Out after your last slot ends. Checking out more than 10 minutes before it ends is recorded as Left Early
Time countedOnly time within your scheduled hours is counted. Time after the scheduled end is not counted
Several slots in a dayCheck in once at the start of the first slot and check out once after the last slot
Leave applicationsAllowed until 10 minutes after your first slot starts
LocationYou must be at the office point, within about 10 metres, to check in or out
Email remindersYou receive a reminder if you have not checked in after the grace period, and another if you have not checked out 10 minutes after your last slot ends
Applying for leave
  1. Open the attendance portal or Remote Leave Link from Google Classroom and tap Apply for Leave. It is available until 10 minutes after your slot starts.
  2. Write your reason in a few keywords(e.g. fever,family emergency,sickness) and tap Send Application.
  3. The coordinators receive your application by email and approve or reject it.
  4. You receive an email with the decision.
  • Pending: wait for the decision. Check-in is not available while it is pending.
  • Approved: the day is recorded as Leave.
  • Rejected: you are expected to attend. Check in as soon as you can, and the late rules apply. If you do not check in, the day is recorded as Absent.
What each status means
On TimeChecked in within the grace period
LateChecked in after the grace period
Left EarlyChecked out before the last slot ended, beyond the grace period
AbsentNo check-in recorded and no approved leave
LeaveLeave approved by the coordinators
Exam DutyListed for exam duty. You can check in and out at any time that day, with no late or early marking
Do's and don'ts
Do
  • Check in and check out yourself at the department office.
  • Use your own East West University Google account.
  • Allow location access and keep it on High accuracy.
  • Open the app in Chrome or Safari.
  • Apply for leave early, with a clear and short reason.
  • Check out before you leave the office.
  • Inform the coordinators or Daniel(2023-2-40-062@std.ewubd.edu) if something goes wrong.
Do not
  • Check in or out for another TA, or let anyone use your account.
    • Sign in with a personal Gmail account.
  • Forget to check out.
  • Wait until the grace period is over to apply for leave.
Quick fixes
  • Location is blocked: allow location for your browser in the phone settings, turn on High accuracy, then reload the page.
  • Blank or white page: turn on and off the Airplane mode to reset your network settings. Then try to reload the page. Make sure you are signed in using your university gmail account.
  • Wrong account: use a private window or a browser profile that has your East West University account.
  • The system is busy: wait a moment and tap again.If you see the same message again,close the tab and delete your recent cache data from the browser and try again.
  • You are too far from the office: move closer to the department office and try again.
  • Still stuck: contact the coordinators or the Senior Departmental Officer using the email at the bottom of this page.
Add the app to your home screen

Open the attendance app first, then add it to your home screen so you can start it with one tap.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the attendance app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app), then tap Add.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open the attendance app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

You may need to sign in again, and your browser will still ask for location access. If the icon opens a blank page, open the app link in your browser again.

Privacy

Privacy and data

What the attendance app records. Your name and university email, your check-in and check-out times, your location at the moment you check in or out, and a random device code that helps keep check-ins reliable. Your location is not tracked at any other time.

Who can see it. TAs see only their own record. The TA coordinators and the system administrator can see all records. Leave reasons are visible only to the coordinators.

What this public page shows. Today's office hours and a basic status for each scheduled TA, by name, such as Checked in, Late or On leave. It never shows emails, check-in times, locations or leave reasons.

Visit statistics. This page counts visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and does not track individual visitors.

Questions or concerns. Contact the email at the bottom of this page.

The attendance app is limited to registered UTAs, GTAs and TA coordinators of the Department of English. Other visitors are asked to sign in and cannot open it.
