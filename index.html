UTA/GTA Digital Attendance Monitor

UTA/GTA Attendance Monitor is the digital system used by the Department of English to record the office-hour attendance of Undergraduate and Graduate Teaching Assistants, handle leave applications, and monitor attendance.

Open the attendance app

Sign in with your East West University Google account.

Access is limited to registered UTAs, GTAs and TA coordinators of the Department of English.
